Source register, scope & complete relationship list

This is a curated public-web map, not an exhaustive organizational chart or a record of private communications. It covers CIMIT’s named team, CINTA’s explicit roster and selected connected institutions, POCTRN centers, RADx/ITAP and agency partnerships. Roles reflect the reviewed pages, which can be stale. Institutional ties do not imply personal contact or reporting lines.

Placeholder names, incomplete biographies and repeated template sections were excluded. Michael Dempsey and Michael K. Dempsey are treated as the same person in the CIMIT/CINTA context. Each pair is counted once. Source repetition is never counted as interaction frequency. Historical founding and launch ties are retained and identified in the evidence.
