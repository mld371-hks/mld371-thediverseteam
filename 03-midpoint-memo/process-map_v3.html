The CoLab process starts with a Sponsor RFP and CIMIT’s solicitation, workflow, and form setup. Applicants complete applications while Managers track progress and reminders support submission. Where an EoI stage exists, CIMIT screens applications and Managers advance selected teams. Managers assign a mix of commercialization, technical, and clinical reviewers, with conflict screening and new-expert recruitment where needed. Individual scores feed reports and Excel summaries for a panel, whose consensus is recorded separately and sent to the Sponsor for funding decisions. CIMIT then notifies teams, provides sanitized feedback, and repeats the process for subsequent stages. The supplied outline is a brief draft rather than a complete specification; elapsed delays and friction classifications remain unconfirmed.

Reviewer output context

The supplied Excel example records one application–reviewer pair per row, identified by Application # and Reviewer #. It includes text and numeric values for Region Fit, IML Fit, Technology Fit, Team Fit, and Overall Recommendation, plus individual reviewer totals, application-wide totals, averages, and rank.

For example, application 1 has reviewer totals of 50, 50, 45, 50, and 47: the report shows a total of 242, an average of 48.4, and rank 1. These scores evaluate applications; they are not historical ratings of reviewer performance. The example does not contain a free-text comments column; compiled reviewer comments are described separately in the process outline. Its fields illustrate one report, rather than fixed criteria for every solicitation.
